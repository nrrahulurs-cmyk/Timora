# /brag Plan: Timora

## Product
- **Name:** Timora
- **Category:** Institutional Academic Operating System & Scheduling Workspace
- **Live URL:** https://timora-one.vercel.app
- **Target Audience:** Timetable Coordinators, Deans, Department Heads, Faculty, Students

## Creative Concept & Angle
- **Angle:** Every timetable change has a ripple effect. When a schedule changes, someone gets double-booked, a laboratory loses power, or a teacher is in two places at once. Timora replaces scattered spreadsheets with an institutional operating system that plans, checks constraints, and resolves clashes before they happen—backed by Timo, a grounded AI Copilot that actually understands academic records.
- **Tone:** `polished` (refined, crisp, modern SaaS motion, high-energy beat, zero generic marketing fluff).
- **Format:** Landscape (1920×1080 @ 30fps).
- **Target Duration:** 20.0 seconds.
